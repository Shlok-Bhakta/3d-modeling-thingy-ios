Each clip shows one action. The teal dots mark the fingers. The arrow is the
app's cursor. Watch both: your fingers can be in one editor while the cursor
sends the gesture to another.

Use the player's fullscreen button when you want a closer look.

## Start with the cursor

### Aim, then tap

Move the cursor onto the cube, then tap anywhere to select it. Tapping the cube
with your finger won't aim the cursor for you.

## Navigate around the cube

These gestures change your view. The cube's location, rotation, and scale stay
the same. To change the object itself, use a modeling tool or gizmo.

### Orbit with two fingers

Drag two fingers to look around the cube.

## Move and rotate the cube itself

### Drag the Move gizmo

Choose the Move tool, then aim at one of its arrows. Tap, put the same finger
down again, hold briefly, and drag. This changes the cube's location.

<video controls playsinline preload="none" aria-label="Move the cube with a gizmo" poster="/3d-modeling-thingy-ios/touch/move-cube-gizmo.webp">

  <a href="/3d-modeling-thingy-ios/touch/move-cube-gizmo.mp4">Watch this clip.</a>
</video>

### Drag a Rotate ring

Choose the Rotate tool and aim at a ring. Use the same tap, hold, and drag
sequence to rotate the cube. The viewing angle stays the same.

## Small gestures that help

### Keep moving past the right edge

The cursor wraps to the left edge. You can continue a long movement without
running out of screen.

### Undo with a quick two-finger tap

The cube returns to its rotation before the last edit. Keep the tap quick.
Holding those two fingers opens a context menu instead.

### Redo with a quick three-finger tap

The rotation comes back. Keep your fingers still: moving three fingers pans
the view instead.

### Open a context menu

Hold two fingers still for about 0.3 seconds, then release. A quick two-finger
tap is Undo, so give this gesture time to become a hold.

<video controls playsinline preload="none" aria-label="Hold two fingers to open the context menu" poster="/3d-modeling-thingy-ios/touch/two-finger-hold.webp">

### Open Search

Tap with four fingers quickly and keep them still. Search opens at the cursor.
Use it to find a command when you don't know which menu contains it.
